## Compute Optimizer
- [Overview](#overview)

### Overview

* AWS `Compute Optimizer` is a free service that uses `ml` to analyze historical utilization metrcis and recommens optimal, cost-effective resource configurations for services like `ec2`, `ebs`, `lambda`, `ecs on fargate`, and rds
    - it evaluates past performance data to predict workload behavior on alternative heardware
    - it flags configurations as over provisioned, under provisioned, or optimized so you can balance cost saving against performance rist
