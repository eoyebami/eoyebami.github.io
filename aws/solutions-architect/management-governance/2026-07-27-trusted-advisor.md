## Trusted Advisor
- [Overview](#overview)
- [Categories](#categories)


### Overview

* AWS `Trusted Advisor` is an online tool that inspects your cloud environment and gives you tips to save money, fix securityholes, and make your apps run faster
    - it checks your settings against official rules and shows a simple status list

### Categories

1. Cost Optimization: finds unused and idle items to lower your bill
2. Security: spots open permissions and missing safeguards
3. Performance: speeds up your apps and systems
4. Fault Tolerance: boosts backup and reliability options
5. Service Limits: tracks your use againt account caps
