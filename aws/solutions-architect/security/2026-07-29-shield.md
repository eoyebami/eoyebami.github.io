## Shield
- [Overview](#overview)
- [Tiers](#tiers)

### Overview

* AWS `Shield` is a managed service that protects applications running in aws from `distributed denial of service (DDoS)` attacks
    - it continuously inspects traffic at the edge of the network to intercept volumetric threats before they reach your backend
    - reimburses scaling costs for protected aws services if an attack causes a traffic surge

### Tiers

* `Standard`: free and enabled by default for all aws customers
    - guards against common L3 and L4 infra attacks on `cloudfront` and `route53`
* `Advanced`: paid subscription ($3k/month with a 1-year commitment) that provides custom L7 mitigations and 24/6 access to `AWS Shield Response Team (SRT)` and financial protection against scaling spikes during `DDoS` attacks
