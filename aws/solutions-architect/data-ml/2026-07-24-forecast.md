## Forecast
- [Overview](#overview)
- [Features](#features)

### Overview

* AWS `Forecast` is a fully managed `ml` service that predicts time-series business metrics
    - uses cases: predicting product demand, resource needs, and finanical trends 
    - it typically generates predictions up to 50% more accurately than traditional statistical methods by automatically examining historical data

### Features

* `Data ingestion`: ingest historical time-series data that is uploaded to `s3`
* `Training and Analysis`: it auto cleans the data, selects the best `ml algo` and trains the predictive model on said data
* `Forecasting`: you define the forecast horizon and the model generates accurate prediction for those future intervals
* `Export`: predictions can be exported back to `s3` and pulled to dashboards and applications
* `Quantiles`: percentiles used to indicate the probability of occurrence of a particular outcome
    - can calculate best and worst case scenerios
